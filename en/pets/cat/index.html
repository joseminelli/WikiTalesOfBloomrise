A cat who lives in Rayy's house. She wanders, rests and reacts to you.

How to get

  1. You find her in Rayy’s house in a short scene where she gets her name and joins the family.

In-game sprite

Behavior

  • Wanders around the house and rests in cozy spots.
  • Reacts when you come close.
  • She has many animations: grooming, scratching, curling up, sprawling and meowing.
