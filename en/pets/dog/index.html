A lonely dog you can adopt. He digs up items, fetches things you drop and sleeps in his doghouse.

How to get

  1. Find the lonely dog and talk to him. A sign to build a doghouse appears.
  2. At the sign, pick the doghouse color and pay the materials and gold.
  3. Talk to him again to adopt him and choose a name.

In-game sprite

Behavior

  • Follows you: trots behind and runs if you get far. Click him to make him stay or follow again.
  • When you stop, he wanders nearby and sniffs random spots.
  • Now and then he digs a hole and can find items.
  • Fetches items dropped on the ground and brings them to you.
  • If you stand still for a long time, he lies down and sleeps next to you.
  • Give him a bone (select the item on the toolbar and click him): he is happy for 20 minutes. Happy, he digs and fetches; sad, he only follows.
  • If you tell him to stay, he goes back to his doghouse and sleeps there.
