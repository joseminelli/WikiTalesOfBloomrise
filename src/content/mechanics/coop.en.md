Two players can share one PC. Player 1 uses keyboard/mouse or any controller; Player 2 joins as a little **plant companion** with its own controller.

- Join or leave with the **J** key, the co-op button in the pause menu, or **SELECT + START** on a controller.
- The camera is shared, so players stay close together.
- Each player has their own inventory and toolbar; loot goes to whoever picks it up.
- Villager friendship is shared. The companion cannot be hurt and does not take part in dialogue choices.
