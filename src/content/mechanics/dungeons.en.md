Dungeons are built from connected rooms with enemies, traps and puzzles, and end with a chest once the enemies are defeated.

- **Traps**: floor spikes and sliding saws.
- **Puzzle rooms**: push coloured boxes onto matching buttons to open the doors.
- **Wave dungeons**: survive successive waves of monsters that get stronger each time, with a gold reward.
- **Modifiers** can change a run: total darkness, double-or-nothing loot, inverted controls (Mirror Curse), the Abyss Wanderer, or a random side effect (blur, confusion, faster, slower, more damage, one life...).

Better dungeons have better loot tables; see each monster's drops on the **Monsters** page.
