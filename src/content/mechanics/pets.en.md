## The cat
A cat lives in Rayy's house and wanders between the kitchen and the bedroom, resting and reacting to you.

## The dog
The dog is a longer story with four stages: **not met, met, doghouse built, adopted**.

- You can **build a doghouse** (it costs items and gold) and choose its colour.
- Once adopted you can **name** your dog. It follows you, roams, fetches, rests and goes home to sleep.
- The dog **digs up items** for you from its own loot table.

## The fairy
A pink ball of light that follows Rayy and lights up the surroundings. She is found in the **Tower of Arcania** once the tower has been restored; a secret code can also unlock her.
