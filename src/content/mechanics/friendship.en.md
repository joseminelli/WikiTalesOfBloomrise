Every villager has a friendship level from 0 to 10 hearts (100 points per heart).

- **Talking** to a villager the first time each day gives +10 points.
- **Gifts**: one gift per villager per day. A loved item gives 80 points, a liked item 45, a neutral one 20 and a disliked one 0. Each character's page lists what they love, like and dislike.
- **Rewards** grow with hearts: shop discounts (for shopkeepers), extra gold and XP on their quests (+25% at 6 hearts, +50% at 8), a permanent health or stamina bonus at level 10, and gifts sent to your **mailbox** every few days.
- Friendship never decays. In co-op it is shared between both players.
