Place a **farm plot** (buy a "dirt pack" or craft one at the Composter from leaves) and plant a seed in it.

- Each crop has a **growth time** and, for many crops, a **regrowth time** after the first harvest and a limited number of harvests.
- Some crops **wither** if you leave them too long.
- Growth is measured in real seconds and continues while the game is closed.

See the **Plants** page for the time, number of harvests and seed of every crop. Harvested crops are ingredients for the Stove, Oven, Jam Maker and Wine Barrel.
