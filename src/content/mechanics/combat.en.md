## Weapons
Swords, axes and pickaxes hit in an arc in front of you; the bow uses arrows; the fishing rod and the metal detector are tools equipped like weapons. Axes and pickaxes are also how you chop trees and break rocks and ore.

## Dash and combo
Dashing costs stamina. Landing hits, defeating enemies and perfect dodges build a **combo** that grants speed, damage and critical-chance bonuses.

## Skill tree
You earn attribute and skill points as you level up. The **Skills** page lists every node, its level requirement and its effect (damage, health, stamina, critical, dodge, regeneration, better drops and a "last resort" that saves you once).

## Potions
Potions give timed buffs: health or stamina regeneration, speed, damage, damage reduction, experience and more. Brew them at the Alchemy Table with monster drops.
