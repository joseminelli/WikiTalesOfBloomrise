Recipes are made at **stations** placed in the world: Workbench, Stove, Oven, Forge, Anvil, Alchemy Table, Wine Barrel, Oil Maker, Jam Maker and Composter.

- Each job takes time. A station can run only a limited number of jobs at once, and finished items must be **collected**.
- Jobs keep progressing in real time, even while the game is closed.
- Some ingredients accept **alternatives** (shown as "or"), for example any oil for fried food.
- Tools and weapons are upgraded step by step on the Anvil: wood, iron, chromite; and swords from the Rayy sword through gold and amethyst to soulmerald.

Browse every recipe on the **Crafting** page.
