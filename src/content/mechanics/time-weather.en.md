## Day and night
A full in-game day lasts only a few real minutes. The clock runs from 00:00 to 24:00 and the light changes between dawn, day, evening and night.

- **Villagers follow daily schedules**: they leave home, work, visit the bar and go to sleep at set hours. See each character's page for their schedule.
- **At night** ghosts roam the forest, some fish only bite after dark, and lamps and windows light up.
- **Sleeping** in a bed skips the rest of the night and starts a new day.

## Weather
Each new day the weather is decided again: **sunny**, **rainy** or **windy**. Rain is roughly a 1-in-4 chance and wind about 1-in-5, but the game avoids long streaks of bad weather and eventually forces a change. Rain creates puddles and reflections.

## Crafting and crops use real time
Crops and crafting jobs progress in real seconds (even while the game is closed), not in in-game days.
